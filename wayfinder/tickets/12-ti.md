## Resolution (as implemented)

Everything above landed, plus TWO discoveries beyond the pre-flight:

1. The occ hook was necessary but not sufficient: even with
   character-forced occupations the SCF is MULTI-BASIN (orbitally
   degenerate open shell). A metastable +11.2 mEh solution (same
   3d2 4s2 occupations) carries a polarized core that smears the
   build_full_mo input window at 1.6e-2 (good basin: ~1e-8). Census:
   the default minao guess and vsap reach the bad basin; atom/huckel
   reliably reach the good one. Fix: `run_rohf(..., occ_e_ref=)` --
   energy gate + guess cycling. Side effect: Ti's good-basin
   casscf_relax sits at 2.3e-8, NOT the Sc fact-17 1e-3 class (that
   baseline is basin/substrate-specific).
2. A THIRD radial anatomy. Raw one-parameter radial rotations ALL rise
   (3d->4d +9.8 mEh at theta=0.05; 4s->5s and 4s->6s likewise) -- like
   Sc, inverting C/Si. The radial rungs' real ~11 mEh gain lives on the
   ORTHOGONALIZED substrate's s slot (converged sa_radial window:
   4s 0.75 + 6s 0.27). Counterfactual measured: relax window +88.1 mEh
   vs raw fold, sa_radial window +77.2 -- the ~11 mEh is a genuine
   window-shape effect in a common fold. (A rotated-but-NONORTHOGONAL
   column drops the energy spuriously -- the ticket-09 bug class; one
   such probe was run and discarded during the session.)

Answers to the open questions:
1. d2 3F tilts DESCEND (-163 uEh at delta=0.1, ~half Sc's depth,
   <L^2> barely moves): the JT class. Consequence realized as
   predicted: sa_radial_pur stops at its flat-gauge floor (1.4e-4/5.3e-4).
2. The swap attractor with an s-slot at 4 valence electrons: sa = the
   EXACT Sc-class 4s<->3p swap (window p = 1/6, s ~ 0); sa_pur = a
   PARTIAL swap (s-slot 2/3 s + 1/3 p, window p ~ 1/18) -- survives
   purification; tests branch on window p-weight.

Predictions verified: raw core percent-level (comm 4.9e-2/2.6e-1,
aniso 7.8e-3) BUT ground <L^2> unshifted (unlike Sc's 6.0001);
relax_pur/sa_relax_pur machine-exact (6.2e-14, 1.4e-13/8.1e-13) with
TI_EXACT_TERMS = {3D:15, 3F:35, 3G:18, 3H:22, 3P:15, 5D:5, 5F:7,
5P:3}; free CASSCF -> L~0 escape (+46 mEh); ground 3F; frac reference
+38 mEh, invariant (septet degenerate to 6.5e-11).

Test-band lesson recorded in fact 21e: leave real headroom on
degenerate-but-numerical quantities (the frac septet's 6.5e-11 vs a
1e-10 bound flaked once) and on basin-mixed <L^2> (sa_pur wandered
12.011..12.025 across fresh ROHF draws).
